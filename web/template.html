How the projection works: 400 simulated futures per travel date. Fares jump in steps of the same size and frequency seen in the last 60 days of real data for these dates. They follow the trend set by the sliders and never fall far below Google's normal range. This gives a range of likely outcomes, not a forecast you can bank on.

Prices are for economy, including taxes, from Google Flights on the date shown. They change many times a day. Always confirm on the airline's site before paying. The advice to book or wait compares today's cheapest fare with Google's normal range and its 60-day low. If a fare stays high, the cut-off is 30 days before departure, because domestic fares in India usually rise sharply in the last 2–3 weeks. Data and code: github.com/rizvonium/flight_track
